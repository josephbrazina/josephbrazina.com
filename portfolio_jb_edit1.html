Background

About me

I'm a Human Factors Engineering M.Eng. student at Virginia Tech with a B.S. in Psychology from JMU, where I graduated Cum Laude. My background sits at the intersection of cognitive science and systems design — understanding not just how people think, but how that thinking breaks down when systems don't account for it.

I'm most interested in contexts where good design genuinely matters: safety-critical systems, complex interfaces, and anywhere that a bad UX decision has real consequences beyond frustration. Whether I'm running a usability study, building Figma mockups, or analyzing experimental data, the goal is the same — fewer errors, less friction, more trust.
